  are licensed for use only and must stay unmodified, branding included
  ([`LICENSE-SKILLS.txt`](LICENSE-SKILLS.txt) sections 3.3 and 3.8).

  attribution page.
- **Circulate generated briefings within your own organization**, and to your
  advisors and processors, subject to your agreement with Zeta.
